# Platform Naming Recommendations: Governed AI Delivery

**Positioning Context:** 
These names are designed to position the platform as the delivery and governance system that sits *on top* of AI coding tools. If AI tools are the raw engine, this platform is the steering, the governor, and the binding structure that ensures enterprise-grade delivery and maintenance.

**Clearance Criteria:**
All names below have passed a preliminary trademark landscape check (no major software/B2B tech collisions found in WIPO/USPTO) and have the exact `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` domains currently **unregistered and available** (exceptions noted inline).

*(Disclaimer: This is a preliminary landscape check. Formal clearance by IP counsel is required before final selection.)*

### 1. Detentiv
**The Metaphor:** The mechanical catch. In mechanical engineering (and watchmaking), a detent is the mechanism that catches and governs a gear, preventing it from spinning out of control.
**The Pitch:** AI generates code at blinding speed. *Detentiv* is the governing mechanism that catches, verifies, and controls that output for the enterprise.
**The Vibe:** Highly engineered, precise, and punchy. It carries the "escapement" metaphor but sounds like a modern B2B SaaS platform.

### 2. Hafnion
**The Metaphor:** The control rod. Hafnium is the premium control-rod material in a nuclear reactor — the element whose job is absorbing neutrons so the chain reaction proceeds at exactly the rate the operators choose. Insert to slow, withdraw to accelerate: continuous, precise governance of an immense force.
**The Pitch:** *Hafnion* is the control rod for AI-built software — the mechanism that makes the reaction dial-able rather than runaway.
**The Vibe:** Element-name gravitas, invented enough to be fully ownable. Sustainment rather than restraint: the mechanism is what makes the power continuously useful.

### 3. Moderon
**The Metaphor:** The moderator. In a reactor, raw fission neutrons are too fast to sustain a chain reaction — the moderator (graphite, heavy water) slows them to exactly the speed where the reaction becomes *sustained and usable*. The moderator doesn't fight the force; it makes the force productive.
**The Pitch:** Raw AI is fast neutrons. *Moderon* is what turns a burst of energy into a power station.
**The Vibe:** The strongest pure metaphor in the list — governance that enables, not brakes. Also carries the everyday "moderation/governance" meaning for free.
*(Note: the exact `.com` is taken, so a modifier like `moderonhq.com` would be required, though `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are available).*

### 4. Wellhead
**The Metaphor:** Harnessing geothermal force. The engineered valve assembly that sits on top of a geothermal bore. Beneath it: superheated, pressurized force from the planet's core that would otherwise vent as a geyser or disaster. The wellhead is the single point where that raw force becomes governed, meterable, productive energy.
**The Pitch:** The power is already down there. *Wellhead* is how you bring it up safely — and keep it flowing for thirty years.
**The Vibe:** Harnessing rather than restraint, with the maintenance/sustained-production story built into the word. Strong New Zealand geothermal resonance (Wairakei was the world's second geothermal power station).
*(Note: the exact `.com` is taken; `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are available).*

### 5. Headrace
**The Metaphor:** Harnessing hydro. The channel that carries water from the reservoir *to* the turbine — the engineered path between stored potential and useful work. (Its sibling, the tailrace, carries the spent flow away.)
**The Pitch:** AI is the reservoir. *Headrace* is the engineered channel that turns potential into delivery.
**The Vibe:** "Head" (pressure, intelligence) + "race" (speed, channel) is a lucky compound — it sounds fast *and* governed.
*(Note: the exact `.com` is taken; `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are available).*

### 6. Tidegate
**The Metaphor:** Harnessing the tide. The gate that lets an unstoppable planetary force flow through on your terms, in your direction, on your schedule.
**The Pitch:** You can't stop the tide. *Tidegate* decides what it drives.
**The Vibe:** Punchy and spellable. "Gate" carries the governance meaning (quality gates, stage gates) for free — mapping directly onto the platform's Gate 1 plan-approval mechanic.
*(Note: the exact `.com` is taken; `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are available).*

### 7. Cinctis
**The Metaphor:** The binding structure. Derived from the Latin *cincture* (to bind, encircle, or gird).
**The Pitch:** AI code generation is sprawling and chaotic. *Cinctis* binds it into a tight, secure, deliverable package.
**The Vibe:** Sharp and technical. It perfectly matches the existing Latin house style (*Omnia*, *Vectis*) while remaining highly ownable.

### 8. Chalinos (kuh-LYE-nos)
**The Metaphor:** The magical bridle. In Greek mythology, Pegasus (a creature of immense power and flight) could not be ridden until Athena gifted the *Chalinos*—the golden bridle that allowed it to be steered.
**The Pitch:** AI is raw, untamed horsepower. *Chalinos* is the technology that lets the enterprise actually steer it.
**The Vibe:** Mythological but highly technical-sounding. A great story-driven name.

### 9. Domitis
**The Metaphor:** The tamer. Derived from the Latin *domitor* (to tame, subdue, or harness).
**The Pitch:** If AI is the wild beast, *Domitis* is the mechanism that harnesses it to do actual, reliable work.
**The Vibe:** Authoritative and secure. Fits seamlessly into the Latin product suite.

### 10. Helmiv
**The Metaphor:** The steering mechanism. A modern, contrived spin on taking the "helm."
**The Pitch:** AI provides the wind and the engine; *Helmiv* provides the direction, governance, and safe arrival.
**The Vibe:** Extremely easy to spell and pronounce. The `-iv` suffix modernizes a classic, universally understood English metaphor.

### 11. Cincture
**The Metaphor:** The binding structure. A real English word meaning a binding, a girdle, or an encircling structure that holds things together.
**The Pitch:** AI code generation is sprawling and loose. *Cincture* binds it into a tight, secure, deliverable package.
**The Vibe:** Classic, structural, and highly ownable in the tech space.
*(Note: While the trademark landscape is clear, the exact `.com` is taken, so a modifier like `cincturehq.com` or `usecincture.com` would be required, though `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are available).*

### 12. Cinctiv
**The Metaphor:** The modern binding. A non-Latin-sounding variant of *Cinctis*.
**The Pitch:** Taking raw, scattered AI outputs and binding them into a cohesive, structural whole.
**The Vibe:** Active and modern. Sounds like a fast-moving tech company while retaining the structural metaphor.

### 13. Frenatis
**The Metaphor:** The controller. Derived from the Latin *frenator* (the controller, the bridle, the rein).
**The Pitch:** AI provides the acceleration; *Frenatis* provides the control.
**The Vibe:** A direct Latin equivalent to the *Chalinos* metaphor. Serious, governed, and enterprise-ready.

### 14. Plumbiv
**The Metaphor:** The source of truth. Derived from the *plumb line*—the heavy weight used since ancient times to establish an exact, true vertical.
**The Pitch:** AI hallucinates and drifts. *Plumbiv* establishes the exact, verifiable truth and ensures the system is structurally sound.
**The Vibe:** Grounded, architectural, and focused on truth/provenance.

### 15. Normiv
**The Metaphor:** The standard. Derived from *norma* (the carpenter's square used to measure right angles).
**The Pitch:** *Normiv* sets the standard, measures the output, and governs the quality of AI-generated systems.
**The Vibe:** Evokes "norms," "standards," and "normalization." Very strong for a governance platform.

### 16. Shkadov (shka-DOFF)
**The Metaphor:** The Shkadov thruster — the megastructure that steers a star. A colossal mirror placed beside a sun reflects its own radiation back at it, turning the star's raw output into thrust and slowly moving the entire solar system in a chosen direction. The star keeps burning exactly as before; the engineering is purely about *direction*.
**The Pitch:** You don't throttle the star. *Shkadov* points it where you want to go.
**The Vibe:** The most audacious metaphor in the list — harnessing-without-restraint at the largest scale physics permits. Instant credibility with technical audiences (Dyson-sphere-adjacent sci-fi pedigree); honours a real scientist (Leonid Shkadov, 1987) rather than borrowing fiction's IP.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear — no software or B2B tech company found under the name; usage is confined to physics literature (the Shkadov thruster, plus unrelated fluid-dynamics equations). ⚠️ The exact `.com` is registered (appears to be a personal-surname holding, not a tech company); `.io` and `.ai` are available, `.dev` needs manual confirmation. ⚠️ Say-spell risk: the `Shk-` consonant cluster is natural in Slavic languages but trips some English speakers — test before committing.

### 17. Claviv (KLAH-viv)
**The Metaphor:** The key, twice over. Latin *clavis* — the key, the thing that grants governed access. And **Christopher Clavius**, the Jesuit mathematician behind the Gregorian calendar reform: the man who took a system that had drifted ten days out of true and brought it back into specification — with a correction mechanism so well-designed it has held for 450 years. (Clavius is also the moon base in *2001: A Space Odyssey*, where humanity quarantines and governs its first contact with a force it doesn't yet understand.)
**The Pitch:** Systems drift. *Claviv* is the correction that holds.
**The Vibe:** Precision, long-horizon maintenance, and quiet institutional authority — reform that outlasts its reformer. Sits naturally in the `-iv` family (Detentiv, Helmiv, Sottiv, Tersiv) and beside the Latin house style (Omnia, Vectis).
**Clearance:** ✅ `.com`, `.io`, and `.ai` all available (`.dev` needs manual confirmation) — one of the few candidates with a near-clean sweep. ⚠️ Flag for IP counsel: no direct collision found, but the `clav-` neighbourhood is busy with small software/security firms (Claviu, Clavi, Clavisi, Clavik, Clavium, Claviz, CLAIV) — the cluster needs a professional confusion-risk read.

## SLM Product Naming: Deliberate Smallness

**Positioning Context:**
A separate naming track for the small language model offering — an environmentally and socially more responsible alternative to LLMs. The image to conjure flips from *mastering a terrifying force* to *deliberate smallness*: small not because it failed to be big, but because right-sized is the wiser, more elegant, more humane choice.

### 1. Sottiv
**The Metaphor:** From *sotto voce* — "under the voice," the musician's hushed register that makes the whole hall lean in. Power expressed through restraint, not volume.
**The Pitch:** Everyone else is shouting at scale. *Sottiv* speaks softly — and says exactly enough.
**The Vibe:** Warm, humane, quietly confident. The `-iv` suffix gives family resemblance to the governed-AI candidates (Detentiv, Helmiv) if a shared house style is wanted.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (nearest neighbour is SOTI, mobile device management — no collision). ✅ `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` all available.

### 2. Tersiv
**The Metaphor:** From Latin *tersus* — polished, clean, precise (the root of "terse," which originally meant *elegantly concise*, not curt).
**The Pitch:** Every word earns its place. *Tersiv*: polished down to exactly what's needed.
**The Vibe:** The most "engineering" of the soft names — fewer tokens, no waste, nothing missing.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (nearest neighbours: "Tersify", a Zurich fintech-AI firm, and "Tersus", a Chinese no-code platform — no direct collision). ✅ `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` all available.

### 3. Susurro
**The Metaphor:** Spanish/Italian for **whisper** (from Latin *susurrus*, one of the language's most beautiful onomatopoeias).
**The Pitch:** The whisper, not the shout — a low-energy model that says what matters.
**The Vibe:** The most evocative of the three; romantic, memorable, effortless to say.
**Clearance:** ⚠️ Usable but flagged for IP counsel: a small open-source macOS voice-dictation tool called `susurro` exists (PyPI/GitHub, commercial arm at susurro.live) running local Whisper/Llama models — adjacent to the local/small-AI space. Also "susuROBO" (Japanese AI avatar firm) and "El Susurro" (Spanish concert-streaming platform). No registered software trademark found. ❌ `.ai` taken; ✅ `.com`, `.io`, and `.dev` available.

### 4. Casimira
**The Metaphor:** From the Casimir effect — *constrain* empty vacuum between two plates and a real, measurable force appears. Constraint literally creates power from nothing.
**The Pitch:** Constraint isn't a compromise. *Casimira*: the force you get by choosing limits.
**The Vibe:** The deepest "small by design" story in physics, softened into a name.
**Clearance:** ⚠️ Moderate risk — flag for IP counsel: **Casimira Partners** (casimirai.com, a consultancy explicitly named after the Casimir effect, with organizational-diagnostics tooling), plus phonetic neighbours **CASYMIR** (Swiss ERP for pharma/food manufacturing) and **CASiMiR** (river-ecosystem simulation software). ❌ `.com` taken; ✅ `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` available.

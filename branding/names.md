# Naming Recommendations: Governed AI Delivery

**Positioning Context:**
These names position the organisation and platform as the delivery and governance system that sits *on top* of AI coding tools. If AI tools are the raw engine, this is the steering, the governor, the gate, and the binding structure that ensures enterprise-grade delivery and maintenance. The mission read used for ranking: **delivery and completion first** (`pitch.md`: "generation is free, completion is the business" — "Clotho spins the thread. Lachesis measures it. Atropos cuts it"), **gating the raw power of AI second** (controlled admission and governed advancement), with deliberate smallness (the SLM offering) as a companion story.

Focus on finding a name that rolls naturally of the tongue, ages well, that will still sound good in 10 years, that communicates solid enterpise-ready, steadfast, trustworthy. Fonterra, Terraform, Oritain all have a certain permanence(?).

There is *no* house style. I just want something that works. I don't want to sound like a Y-Combinator startup from 2026. I want to sound like a 100-year-old advisory firm or a premium design studio.

**Clearance Criteria:**
Names marked ✅ clean passed a preliminary trademark landscape check (no major software/B2B tech collisions found) and had the exact `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` domains **unregistered and available** at their sweep (June 2026 unless noted). Partial positions are stated per-TLD. Domain availability is point-in-time — Tidegate lost its `.io` between sweeps.

*(Disclaimer: This is a preliminary landscape check. Formal clearance by IP counsel is required before final selection.)*

## Name Details (in rank order)

### 1. Contrafactum — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** In musicology, a *contrafactum* is the practice of taking an existing, often chaotic or secular melody, and writing completely new, governed, purposeful lyrics over the top of it—without changing the underlying structure. Generative AI provides the raw, sprawling melody; *Contrafactum* imposes the meaning, the governance, and the enterprise structure that makes it usable.
**The Pitch:** AI generates the raw melody. *Contrafactum* writes the words that make it mean something.
**The Vibe:** Academic, deeply historical, and highly structured. It carries the Latinate house style (*Omnia*, *Vectis*) while perfectly describing the act of taking a raw output and imposing a governed, purposeful structure over it.
**Clearance:** ✅ **Verified clean of marks in software/AI (June 2026).** No `Contrafactum` trademark in any USPTO software class. The only same-spelling uses are a small PA financial advisory firm (Contrafactum Research & Management) and a 2024 AR music app for a conference. **Domains:** ✅ `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` all unregistered and available; ❌ `.com` registered (NameBright parked/resale).


### 2. Boundstone (BOUND-stone) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** Two meanings in one grounded word. A *boundstone* marks a sanctioned boundary — the agreed limit, which is exactly what a governance-and-gating layer enforces; and in geology a *boundstone* is reef rock whose organisms *bind* loose sediment into one rigid mass (the same binding idea as Cinctis). Add the everyday "bound to" — committed, obligated — and the word carries boundary, binding, and commitment at once.
**The Pitch:** Generation sprawls. *Boundstone* sets the line it builds to — and binds it there.
**The Vibe:** Solid, grounded, faintly geological; a Treadstone-style stone-compound without the *Bourne* (or Treadstone 71) baggage. Sits in the *Drystone*/*Copestone* register and pairs the gating story (boundary) with the packaging story (binding).
**Clearance:** ✅ **Verified clean (June 2026).** No `Boundstone` mark in USPTO classes 9 or 42; `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are all unregistered and available (`whois` "Domain not found" + empty `dig NS`); `.com` is parked for resale on HugeDomains (acquirable at a premium, like Tidegate's). Out-of-class only: UK building firms (**Boundstone Developments**, `boundstone.co.uk`). ⚠️ Minor flags for IP counsel: **Bondstone** is a registered mark but in Class 33 (whiskey — unrelated goods), and an unregistered hobby open-source "boundstone" Minecraft server exists.


### 3. Copestone (COPE-stone) — platform

**The Metaphor:** The copestone (or coping) is the finishing top course of a drystone wall — the crowning stones that complete the structure and shield everything beneath from weather. Generation raises the courses; the copestone is what finishes and seals the wall. It maps directly onto the completion thesis — *generation is free, completion is the business*.
**The Pitch:** Generation lays the courses. *Copestone* sets the cap that finishes the wall.
**The Vibe:** Earthy and architectural, in the *Drystone* register; carries "completion/finishing" for free and reads as solid, finished craft. A touch more of a mouthful than the punchier marks.
**Clearance:** ✅ **No registered trademark found** in USPTO classes 9 or 42 (June 2026) — the only same-spelling users are services firms, not software products. **Domains:** `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are unregistered and available; `.com` is held by **Telepathy Inc** (a premium domain investor — `copestone.com` redirects to telepathy.com), so it is acquirable but likely pricey, not in third-party product use. ⚠️ Flag for IP counsel: the live in-class adjacency — **Copestone** is a UK B2B **content-marketing agency** that explicitly serves ICT/IT/software-development clients (a common-law, services-class presence; confusion-risk read needed) — alongside out-of-class users (**Copestone General Contractors**, a Colorado building firm since 1985; **Copestone Wealth Management**, London).


### 4. Plinthstone (PLINTH-stone) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** In classical architecture, a plinth is the heavy base or lower square block of a column, pedestal, or wall. The "plinth stone" is the foundational block upon which the entire vertical structure rests. AI builds the flashy superstructure (the column and capital); Specify provides the *Plinthstone* that keeps it from sinking into the mud or toppling over.
**The Pitch:** AI builds the superstructure. *Plinthstone* provides the foundation that keeps it standing.
**The Vibe:** Architectural, permanent, classical. It sounds like a 100-year-old institution or a high-end structural engineering firm. It carries the permanence of the `-stone` suffix but avoids the trademark crowding of common terms like Keystone or Capstone.
**Clearance:** ✅ **Verified clean of marks in software/AI (June 2026).** The only existing use of the word is a defunct Indian real estate advisory firm (Plinthstone REMA) that was acquired in 2022. There is zero overlap with international software, AI, or developer tools. **Domains:** ✅ `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are all completely unregistered and available right now.


### 5. Enantia (en-AN-tee-uh) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** From the Greek *enantios*, meaning opposite or against. The balancing, opposing force. If generative AI is the chaotic, infinite forward rush, *Enantia* is the structural counter-force that pushes back just enough to hold it in check and make it useful.
**The Pitch:** AI is the chaotic forward rush. *Enantia* is the governed return.

**The Pitch:** You can't stop the tide of generation. *Countertide* is the force that governs its flow.
**The Vibe:** Natural, powerful, physical infrastructure. It sounds highly engineered and deliberate, like a massive civil works project that tames a wild river.
**Clearance:** ✅ **Verified clean of marks in software/AI (June 2026).** No `Countertide` trademark in any USPTO software class. ### 7. Libratic — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** Extracted from *Libra* (the scale or balance) and given the scientific `-ic` suffix. It describes the physical, mechanical action of balancing a system. If generative AI is a wildly fluctuating output, *Libratic* is the mechanism that enforces the scale and holds it steady.
**The Pitch:** Generation drifts. *Libratic* enforces the balance.
**The Vibe:** Sharp, kinetic, and highly scientific. It sounds like an unbreakable law of physics or a high-end control systems engineering firm.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (no registered marks in software/B2B tech). ✅ `**.com` is available** (parked for $1500 USD), along with `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` wide open. An incredibly clean, highly ownable runway.


### 8. Equilibric — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** In control theory and physics, equilibrium is the state where all competing forces are perfectly balanced and stable. Generative AI is inherently unbalanced, prone to hallucination and drift. *Equilibric* is the mechanism that enforces the balance.
**The Pitch:** AI is a runaway reaction. *Equilibric* is the governor.
**The Vibe:** It sounds extremely precise, quantitative, and deeply scientific. It feels like a high-end algorithmic trading firm or an aerospace control-systems contractor. Because it is a morphological shift of a known English word rather than a portmanteau, it has zero learning curve.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (no registered marks in software/B2B tech). ✅ `**.com` is unregistered and available** (June 2026), along with `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev`. An absolute unicorn for a 4-syllable dictionary-adjacent word.


### 9. Annealic (uh-NEE-lik) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** From *anneal* — the metallurgical process of heating a material and letting it settle, defect by defect, into its strongest, lowest-energy, most stable crystalline state. *Simulated annealing* borrows the same idea to drive a chaotic search down to its global optimum. A generative system runs hot and disordered; *Annealic* is the controlled cool-down that settles it into the one stable, defect-free configuration that ships.
**The Pitch:** Generation runs hot and disordered. *Annealic* settles it into its strongest stable state.
**The Vibe:** Scientific, precise, and quietly industrial — a coined `-ic` adjective that sits exactly beside Equilibric and Libratic as a "fundamental property" name. Zero AI-trend register; it sounds like a material science discovered, not a startup founded.
**Clearance:** ✅ **Clean sweep — `.com`, `.io`, and `.ai` all unregistered and available** (June 2026; verisign "No match" + registry "Domain not found"); `.dev` needs manual confirmation. ✅ Trademark landscape clear: no "Annealic" entity found anywhere — the only neighbour is the unrelated bare word *Anneal* (a now-dissolved Belfast hardware-collaboration tool), a different word in a different class.


### 10. Drystead (DRY-sted) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** A coined compound, never a dictionary word — *dry* (drystone construction: no mortar, no adhesive; structure from gravity and precise fit, not glue) + *stead* (a homestead/farmstead; "stand in good stead" — a rooted place that endures and outlasts its builder). It keeps the whole drystone image — small pieces, deliberately placed, holding for decades without glue — while reading as a single ownable mark rather than the (taken) common word *Drystone*.
**The Pitch:** Everyone else pours glue. *Drystead* is built dry — to stand for decades.
**The Vibe:** Earthy, plain-spoken, durable; zero AI register — the rare Cursor/Linear-register name that survived the survey, and unlike *Drystone* it carries no incumbent. The deterministic-governance story (gravity and fit, not adhesive hope) and the permanence story in one word.
**Clearance:** ✅ **Verified clean (June 2026).** All four exact TLDs are unregistered and available — `drystead.com` (Verisign "No match"), plus `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` (registry "Domain not found" + empty `dig NS`); the only name in the grounded register with the `.com` free to register today.

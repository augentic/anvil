# Naming Recommendations: Governed AI Delivery

**Positioning Context:**
These names position the organisation and platform as the delivery and governance system that sits *on top* of AI coding tools. If AI tools are the raw engine, this is the steering, the governor, the gate, and the binding structure that ensures enterprise-grade delivery and maintenance. The mission read used for ranking: **delivery and completion first** (`pitch.md`: "generation is free, completion is the business" — "Clotho spins the thread. Lachesis measures it. Atropos cuts it"), **gating the raw power of AI second** (controlled admission and governed advancement), with deliberate smallness (the SLM offering) as a companion story.

Focus on finding a name that rolls naturally of the tongue, ages well, that will still sound good in 10 years, that communicates solid enterpise-ready, steadfast, trustworthy. Fonterra, Terraform, Oritain all have a certain permanence(?).

There is *no* house style. I just want something that works. I don't want to sound like a Y-Combinator startup from 2026. I want to sound like a 100-year-old advisory firm or a premium design studio.

**Clearance Criteria:**
Names marked ✅ clean passed a preliminary trademark landscape check (no major software/B2B tech collisions found) and had the exact `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` domains **unregistered and available** at their sweep (June 2026 unless noted). Partial positions are stated per-TLD. Domain availability is point-in-time — Tidegate lost its `.io` between sweeps.

*(Disclaimer: This is a preliminary landscape check. Formal clearance by IP counsel is required before final selection.)*

**The Pitch:** AI generates the raw melody. *Contrafactum* writes the words that make it mean something.

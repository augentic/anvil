# Senior software practitioners for systems that matter

Propellerhead is a New Zealand software-services company. Since 2002, we have worked with government, commercial, and non-profit organisations to design, build, operate, and evolve complex software.

Our next chapter is focused on building and changing critical systems deliberately: recovering what matters in existing estates, giving new systems a considered foundation, and preserving infrastructure options over their life.

## Why this work

The obstacle is rarely a shortage of target technologies or code-writing capacity. Existing systems accumulate uncertainty:

- behaviour that exists but is not documented;
- documentation that no longer matches production;
- business rules distributed across systems and teams;
- dependencies that become visible only when they fail;
- decisions whose original context has disappeared;
- tests that provide activity without independent assurance.

New systems face the inverse risk: implementation arrives before people have reflected on intent, consequences, acceptance, or infrastructure coupling.

Both conditions reward disciplined discovery, senior judgment, and accountable delivery. They cannot be solved by generating code faster.

## What we bring

### Delivery experience

We have built and supported software where reliability, public impact, integration complexity, and long-term operation matter.

### Senior, cross-functional teams

Our work combines product analysis, system archaeology, architecture, software engineering, verification, delivery leadership, and operations. Specialists work around the outcome rather than handing it across disconnected phases.

### A method designed for uncertainty

We keep source evidence distinct, preserve provenance, expose disagreement, and make authority explicit. Programmes proceed through bounded outcomes rather than a single irreversible rewrite.

### Infrastructure portability

For appropriate systems, Omnia separates WebAssembly application components from host-selected infrastructure backends. Each invocation runs in a fresh isolated instance, while persistent state and external effects remain behind host capabilities. Supported backends can change without changing or recompiling guest application logic, preserving more client choice over where and how the system runs.

### Purpose-built delivery infrastructure

We are developing Emery to make evidence-backed delivery repeatable. Today it coordinates bounded source evidence, provenance, reviewable plans and specifications, exact execution inputs, isolated build and verification, and durable change facts.

Broader estate discovery, detached multi-repository delivery, publication verification, and protected behavioural replay remain development goals. Engagements state which controls are supplied by Emery, which are supplied by Propellerhead's delivery practice, and which remain in the client's environment.

Emery supports the practice; it does not replace the practitioners, client stakeholders, or governance needed to deliver a critical programme.

## Our operating principles

### Tell the truth about what is known

An explicit unknown is safer than a plausible invention. Confidence must come from evidence and verification, not presentation quality.

### Preserve accountability

Tools and agents may perform work. People and organisations remain accountable for intent, delivery, and acceptance.

### Make room for reflection

Review is not ceremony around the “real work” of producing code. It is where practitioners and stakeholders examine assumptions, consequences, and trade-offs while decisions remain reversible.

### Welcome open access to capable tools

Open-source models and agentic coding tools will democratise software production. We do not treat access to an agent as our moat. We compete on judgment, context, delivery discipline, portability, verification, and accountability.

### Deliver before certainty becomes theatre

We seek the smallest responsible boundary that can produce an accepted result. We do not require complete knowledge of a large estate before beginning useful work.

### Leave the client stronger

The client retains its code, artifacts, evidence, and decisions. Each engagement should improve the organisation's ability to understand, operate, and change its systems and infrastructure.

### Prefer continuity to lock-in

Clients should continue working with us because accumulated understanding and a maturing practice make the next change better—not because leaving is technically difficult.

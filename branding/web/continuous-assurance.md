# Keep the system understandable as it evolves

A new build or modernization programme should not produce a system that immediately begins accumulating the same uncertainty and infrastructure coupling as the one it replaces.

Continuous assurance keeps the accepted account of the system current as regulation, products, integrations, teams, and operating conditions change.

## From project artifact to operational asset

During definition, readiness, and delivery, the organisation accumulates:

- behavioural specifications connected to source evidence;
- decisions about conflicts and intended behaviour;
- verification profiles, fixtures, captures, and checks;
- known gaps and deferred debt;
- system and dependency topology;
- infrastructure capability and backend decisions;
- a history of accepted changes.

These assets are valuable only while they remain aligned with the product. We maintain them as part of subsequent delivery rather than treating them as a one-off documentation exercise.

## What the ongoing relationship covers

### Bounded product and regulatory change

New requirements enter the same evidence-backed rhythm. The existing baseline narrows discovery, makes impact clearer, and exposes where a new request conflicts with accepted behaviour.

### Behavioural baseline maintenance

Specifications and supporting evidence are refreshed when the system, its operating environment, or authoritative intent changes.

### Verification improvement

Weak or missing verification paths become a managed improvement programme. High-value behaviours can gain stronger independent checks and fixtures over time; behavioural replay is added only where the estate and execution controls can support it honestly.

### Infrastructure portability

For Omnia systems, capability requirements and backend choices remain explicit. Planned substitutions can be exercised against the same guest application logic, while provider-specific data and operational concerns remain visible.

### Decision and debt management

Deferred uncertainty remains visible with its origin, reason, and age. Decisions retain the context needed to revisit them responsibly.

### Readiness reassessment

The estate is periodically reassessed as dependencies, teams, platforms, and governance requirements change. Readiness is a changing operational property, not a certificate issued once.

### Governance integration

Where useful, delivery evidence can enter the client's existing CI, release, security, observability, risk, and approval processes.

## Why continuity matters

The first modernization wave bears the cost of recovering context. Each accepted wave should reduce the cost and uncertainty of the next one.

Continuity creates:

- faster impact analysis;
- clearer acceptance boundaries;
- less dependence on individual memory;
- earlier visibility of conflicts and verification gaps;
- a defensible record for assurance and regulatory stakeholders;
- more predictable delivery without rebuilding understanding from zero.

## No subscription disguised as consulting

The client owns the accumulated code, artifacts, evidence, and delivery record. The relationship continues when Propellerhead can deliver the next bounded change more safely and efficiently because the shared baseline and practice have improved.

The value is continuity, not technical lock-in.

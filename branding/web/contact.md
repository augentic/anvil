# Tell us about the critical system you need to build or change

The most useful first conversation is about the outcome, the people who depend on it, and the decisions preventing a responsible commitment.

You do not need a completed business case or replacement architecture.

## Helpful context

If you can, tell us:

- what the system enables and who depends on it;
- whether this is a new system, modernization, or ongoing change;
- why action is necessary now;
- what has made previous change difficult;
- which deadlines, obligations, or operational risks matter;
- where intent or knowledge of the system currently lives;
- whether a modernization or replacement has already been attempted;
- which infrastructure choices the organisation wants to preserve or avoid locking in;
- what decision you need to make next.

We will use the first conversation to assess whether readiness, a bounded modernization wave, or another form of help is appropriate.

## A good fit

We are most useful when the system is important, the decisions deserve reflection, and the organisation needs a defensible path to action. For existing systems, that often means incomplete understanding. For new systems, it often means long-lived consequences or a need for infrastructure optionality.

If the work is a straightforward greenfield build or a well-specified replacement, we will say so. Our deliberate-delivery method should be used where it changes the risk—not added as ceremony.

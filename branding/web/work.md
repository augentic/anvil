## The proof our critical-systems proposition requires

Complex software experience establishes credibility, but it does not by itself prove the new promise around deliberate delivery, human judgment, and infrastructure portability.

The next case studies should show:

- the initial uncertainty and why it prevented a responsible commitment;
- which evidence sources were recovered;
- a material conflict or unknown and how it was handled;
- how readiness changed scope, sequence, price, or the decision to proceed;
- the acceptance boundary for the modernization wave;
- what verification independently supported;
- what behaviour was preserved, intentionally changed, or deferred;
- which important decision changed through stakeholder reflection;
- where Omnia kept application logic unchanged across an infrastructure-backend substitution;
- measured delivery and operational outcomes;
- what the client could understand or change afterwards that it could not before.

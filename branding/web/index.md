# Build and modernize critical systems safely

When a critical system needs to change, but nobody fully understands how it works anymore, a conventional rewrite is a massive gamble. 

Propellerhead helps you safely modernize these systems. We figure out what your system actually does, agree with your team on what to keep, and deliver the new system in safe, clear steps.

We apply this exact same discipline to building new, high-stakes systems—ensuring you don't rush into decisions that become tomorrow's legacy traps.

**Primary action:** Start with a readiness engagement  
**Secondary action:** Talk to us about a critical system

## The hard part isn't writing code

For old systems, the real rules are scattered across outdated docs, messy code, and the memories of a few key people. For new systems, the challenge is making sure you actually understand the goals and constraints before you start building.

Writing code is getting easier and faster thanks to AI. But deciding *what* is worth building, understanding the consequences, and accepting responsibility for the result—that is still human work.

A process that hides disagreement or skips planning creates false certainty. We make the unknowns explicit, help your team make the right decisions, and turn those decisions into a clear delivery plan.

## From evidence to an agreed result

### 1. Figure out what the system must do
For a new system, we start with your goals, constraints, and acceptance criteria. For an old system, we dig into the code, the docs, the tests, and the people who run it to find out how it really works.

### 2. Consider the consequences
We create time for your team to question assumptions, resolve confusion, and consider the long-term impacts of technical decisions. Thinking isn't a delay; it's how you build well.

### 3. Build in safe steps
We break the work down into clear phases. Each phase has a specific goal, known evidence, and clear tests for success. 

### 4. Prove the result
We connect every requirement to the code we write and the tests we run. You can see exactly what changed, why it changed, and what we checked.

## Is this for you?

Our approach is a strong fit when:
- The system is critical to your business or the public.
- Nobody fully understands how the current system works.
- You have to change because of a vendor exit, a new regulation, or an aging platform.
- A previous attempt to rewrite the system failed.
- You are building a new system and want to avoid being locked into one cloud provider.
- You need a defensible, evidence-backed plan before you spend money.

If you just need a simple app or a quick prototype, you don't need us. We are built for the hard stuff.

## Keep your infrastructure options open

When we build applications using our Omnia runtime, your business logic is kept completely separate from your infrastructure. 

This means you can swap out your database, messaging system, or cloud provider later *without* rewriting your core application. It doesn't make migrations magic, but it dramatically reduces the cost and risk of changing your infrastructure in the future.

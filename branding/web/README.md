# Candidate website copy

This directory is a prose-first candidate for a Propellerhead website refresh. It positions the firm around deliberate delivery of new and existing critical systems rather than around an AI tool, a software framework, or a list of technologies. Modernization remains the primary market wedge.

## Proposed navigation

- [Home](index.md) — state the problem, promise, and three-stage offer.
- [Modernization](modernization.md) — describe the flagship service and commercial shape.
- [New systems](new-systems.md) — show how the same discipline applies before a system has legacy behaviour.
- [Readiness](readiness.md) — make the paid entry engagement the primary call to action.
- [How we work](how-we-work.md) — explain evidence, authority, bounded waves, verification, and ownership.
- [Infrastructure portability](infrastructure-portability.md) — make Omnia's guest/host separation and client value explicit.
- [Continuous assurance](continuous-assurance.md) — show the relationship after the first accepted delivery.
- [Work](work.md) — retain the current public portfolio and define the proof required for the new proposition.
- [Our company](company.md) — connect the proposition to Propellerhead's history and delivery posture.
- [Contact](contact.md) — qualify a useful first conversation.

The existing work and case-study material should remain available, but it needs an evidence pass before being rewritten around this proposition. The new site should not imply that prior projects used the current modernization method unless that can be demonstrated.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** build or change a critical system without losing what matters or hard-coding today's infrastructure choices.
2. **Method:** establish or recover what the system must do, reflect on consequences with accountable people, and deliver in bounded waves.
3. **Reason to believe:** make evidence, stakeholder decisions, reviewed specifications, verification, and the agreed result traceable.
4. **Enablers:** use senior practitioners and Emery to make the method repeatable; use Omnia to separate application logic from supported infrastructure backends.

Do not reverse this hierarchy. “Agentic development,” “audit trails,” Emery, and Omnia are mechanisms, not the opening promise.

## Claim discipline

- Say **critical** or **poorly understood**, not “legacy” everywhere. Age is not the problem; unsafe change is.
- Say **demonstrate**, **trace**, or **verify against declared checks**. Do not claim that all behaviour is proven or that risk is eliminated.
- Say **bounded** and **outcome-priced where evidence supports it**. Do not promise a fixed price before readiness establishes the boundary.
- Say **AI-assisted** when relevant. Accountability remains with Propellerhead and the client, not an agent.
- Treat reflection, review, and stakeholder judgment as productive delivery work, not friction apologetically retained around automation.
- Say open source will broaden access to agentic coding; do not imply that using agents is scarce or proprietary.
- Describe Omnia portability precisely: supported backends can be swapped in the host without changing or recompiling guest application logic. Do not promise automatic data migration, identical service semantics, or zero-cost cloud movement.
- Do not claim Emery is open source until the repositories are public.
- Do not claim protected behavioural replay is generally available until the conservation and host-attested verification work has landed and been exercised on a client engagement.
- Distinguish service controls from engine guarantees. Human reviews, approvals, and change control are engagement terms; Emery exposes review seams but does not attest that a person used them.
- Describe broad estate discovery, detached multi-repository execution, publication verification, and protected replay as roadmap until they have landed and been exercised.

## Proof still required

Before publication, replace generic credibility language with evidence:

- one readiness engagement showing how uncertainty changed scope or price;
- one modernization wave with before/after acceptance measures;
- one example of behaviour caught by evidence or independent verification before release;
- one client-approved account of decision traceability;
- one Omnia example that substitutes an infrastructure backend without changing guest application logic;
- the client-approved quotation or account identifying infrastructure portability as a significant benefit;
- measured delivery time, defect, intervention, and conservation outcomes;
- approved client names, quotations, and programme descriptions.

Commercial decisions are also required before publication: a typical readiness duration and price range, minimum client participation, and the standard treatment of scope changes.

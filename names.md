# Naming Recommendations: Governed AI Delivery

**Positioning Context:**
These names position the organisation and platform as the delivery and governance system that sits *on top* of AI coding tools. If AI tools are the raw engine, this is the steering, the governor, the gate, and the binding structure that ensures enterprise-grade delivery and maintenance. The mission read used for ranking: **delivery and completion first** (`pitch.md`: "generation is free, completion is the business" — "Clotho spins the thread. Lachesis measures it. Atropos cuts it"), **gating the raw power of AI second** (controlled admission and governed advancement), with deliberate smallness (the SLM offering) as a companion story.

**Clearance Criteria:**
Names marked ✅ clean passed a preliminary trademark landscape check (no major software/B2B tech collisions found) and had the exact `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` domains **unregistered and available** at their sweep (June 2026 unless noted). Partial positions are stated per-TLD. Domain availability is point-in-time — Tidegate lost its `.io` between sweeps.

*(Disclaimer: This is a preliminary landscape check. Formal clearance by IP counsel is required before final selection.)*

## Name Details (in rank order)

### 1. Libratic — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** Extracted from *Libra* (the scale or balance) and given the scientific `-ic` suffix. It describes the physical, mechanical action of balancing a system. If generative AI is a wildly fluctuating output, *Libratic* is the mechanism that enforces the scale and holds it steady.
**The Pitch:** Generation drifts. *Libratic* enforces the balance.
**The Vibe:** Sharp, kinetic, and highly scientific. It sounds like an unbreakable law of physics or a high-end control systems engineering firm.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (no registered marks in software/B2B tech). ✅ **`.com` is available** (parked for $1500 USD), along with `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` wide open. An incredibly clean, highly ownable runway.

### 2. Equilibric — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** In control theory and physics, equilibrium is the state where all competing forces are perfectly balanced and stable. Generative AI is inherently unbalanced, prone to hallucination and drift. *Equilibric* is the mechanism that enforces the balance.
**The Pitch:** AI is a runaway reaction. *Equilibric* is the governor.
**The Vibe:** It sounds extremely precise, quantitative, and deeply scientific. It feels like a high-end algorithmic trading firm or an aerospace control-systems contractor. Because it is a morphological shift of a known English word rather than a portmanteau, it has zero learning curve.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (no registered marks in software/B2B tech). ✅ **`.com` is unregistered and available** (June 2026), along with `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev`. An absolute unicorn for a 4-syllable dictionary-adjacent word.

### 3. Annealic (uh-NEE-lik) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** From *anneal* — the metallurgical process of heating a material and letting it settle, defect by defect, into its strongest, lowest-energy, most stable crystalline state. *Simulated annealing* borrows the same idea to drive a chaotic search down to its global optimum. A generative system runs hot and disordered; *Annealic* is the controlled cool-down that settles it into the one stable, defect-free configuration that ships.
**The Pitch:** Generation runs hot and disordered. *Annealic* settles it into its strongest stable state.
**The Vibe:** Scientific, precise, and quietly industrial — a coined `-ic` adjective that sits exactly beside Equilibric and Libratic as a "fundamental property" name. Zero AI-trend register; it sounds like a material science discovered, not a startup founded.
**Clearance:** ✅ **Clean sweep — `.com`, `.io`, and `.ai` all unregistered and available** (June 2026; verisign "No match" + registry "Domain not found"); `.dev` needs manual confirmation. ✅ Trademark landscape clear: no "Annealic" entity found anywhere — the only neighbour is the unrelated bare word *Anneal* (a now-dissolved Belfast hardware-collaboration tool), a different word in a different class.

### 4. Tidegate — platform

**The Metaphor:** Harnessing the tide. The gate that lets an unstoppable planetary force flow through on your terms, in your direction, on your schedule.
**The Pitch:** You can't stop the tide. *Tidegate* decides what it drives.
**The Vibe:** Punchy and spellable. "Gate" carries the governance meaning (quality gates, stage gates) for free — mapping directly onto the platform's Gate 1 plan-approval mechanic. Under the gating mission it is the thesis itself, and the June 2026 trademark re-check cleared the field.
**Clearance (trademark re-checked June 2026 — better than first feared):** ✅ **No registered trademark exists on "Tidegate" / "Tide Gate"** in any register surveyed — USPTO usage is confined to patents on the physical hydraulic devices, and generic-in-hydraulics does not block a software-class registration (arbitrary use). ⚠️ One unregistered in-category user: **Tide Gate Systems** (TideGate.co) — a solo US "fractional AI ops" service for local SMBs; no registered mark, no funding found, and the founder's recent posts brand the venture **Signal Harbor**, suggesting a rebrand in progress. Any common-law rights would be narrow (local-business ops services). **Tidegate Capital** (Massachusetts multifamily real estate) and the UK/Panama shelf companies are different classes.
**Domains:** ✅ **`tidegate.ai` and `tidegate.dev` PURCHASED (June 2026) — we hold these.** ❌ `.com` parked for sale on HugeDomains ($8,800 — acquisition candidate); ❌ `.io` registered by a third party (lost between sweeps — Cloudflare-hosted).

### 5. Hafnion — platform

**The Metaphor:** The control rod. Hafnium is the premium control-rod material in a nuclear reactor — the element whose job is absorbing neutrons so the chain reaction proceeds at exactly the rate the operators choose. Insert to slow, withdraw to accelerate: continuous, precise governance of an immense force.
**The Pitch:** *Hafnion* is the control rod for AI-built software — the mechanism that makes the reaction dial-able rather than runaway.
**The Vibe:** Element-name gravitas, invented enough to be fully ownable. Sustainment rather than restraint: the mechanism is what makes the power continuously useful. Scores on both mission axes — a control rod literally gates neutron flux while sustaining the reaction.

### 6. Atropiv (AT-roh-piv) — org

**The Metaphor:** Atropos, the Fate who cuts the thread — literally *a-tropos*, "she who cannot be turned." The cut is final; the decision ships and cannot be unwound.
**The Pitch:** AI coding gave you infinite Clotho. *Atropiv* is the cut that ships.
**The Vibe:** The only candidate that *is* the elevator line rather than alluding to it — anyone who has heard the pitch needs no explanation. The double etymology (cut / irreversible) maps directly onto the accountability story: approved, traced, auditable forever. Sits naturally in the `-iv` family (Completiv, Detentiv, Sottiv, Tersiv).
**Clearance:** ✅ Clean 4-TLD sweep (`.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, `.dev`) — verified independently by two methods (registry-direct RDAP and `whois` "No match" + `dig NS`). ⚠️ Flag for IP counsel: **AtroPIM** (open-source PIM, AtroCore GmbH) is one letter away in spelling, and **Atropos Health** (a funded healthcare-AI company) draws on the same Fate — both need a confusion-risk read.

### 7. Sartoriv (sar-TOR-iv) — org

**The Metaphor:** From Latin *sartor* (tailor) + the house `-iv` suffix. Taking vast yardages of raw, undifferentiated fabric and cutting, fitting, and shaping them to an exact, bespoke human specification.
**The Pitch:** AI gives you infinite cloth. *Sartoriv* cuts the suit.
**The Vibe:** Latinate, elegant, very "enterprise bespoke." It fits the existing `-iv` family (Completiv, Detentiv) perfectly while introducing a deeply human, craft-driven image.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear. ✅ `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` all available.

### 8. Moderon — platform

**The Metaphor:** The moderator. In a reactor, raw fission neutrons are too fast to sustain a chain reaction — the moderator (graphite, heavy water) slows them to exactly the speed where the reaction becomes *sustained and usable*. The moderator doesn't fight the force; it makes the force productive.
**The Pitch:** Raw AI is fast neutrons. *Moderon* is what turns a burst of energy into a power station.
**The Vibe:** The strongest pure metaphor in the list — governance that enables, not brakes; under the gating mission, the thesis itself. Also carries the everyday "moderation/governance" meaning for free.
*(Note: the exact `.com` is taken, so a modifier like `moderonhq.com` would be required, though `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` are available).*

### 9. Detentiv — platform

**The Metaphor:** The mechanical catch. In mechanical engineering (and watchmaking), a detent is the mechanism that catches and governs a gear, preventing it from spinning out of control.
**The Pitch:** AI generates code at blinding speed. *Detentiv* is the governing mechanism that catches, verifies, and controls that output for the enterprise.
**The Vibe:** Highly engineered, precise, and punchy. It carries the "escapement" metaphor but sounds like a modern B2B SaaS platform. Its old restraint-first penalty evaporates once gating joins the mission.

### 10. Tersiv — SLM

**The Metaphor:** From Latin *tersus* — polished, clean, precise (the root of "terse," which originally meant *elegantly concise*, not curt).
**The Pitch:** Every word earns its place. *Tersiv*: polished down to exactly what's needed.
**The Vibe:** The most "engineering" of the soft names — fewer tokens, no waste, nothing missing.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (nearest neighbours: "Tersify", a Zurich fintech-AI firm, and "Tersus", a Chinese no-code platform — no direct collision). ✅ `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` all available.

### 11. Sottiv — SLM

**The Metaphor:** From *sotto voce* — "under the voice," the musician's hushed register that makes the whole hall lean in. Power expressed through restraint, not volume.
**The Pitch:** Everyone else is shouting at scale. *Sottiv* speaks softly — and says exactly enough.
**The Vibe:** Warm, humane, quietly confident. The `-iv` suffix gives family resemblance to the governed-AI candidates (Detentiv, Tersiv) if a shared house style is wanted. The image to conjure for the SLM offering: small not because it failed to be big, but because right-sized is the wiser, more elegant, more humane choice.
**Clearance:** ✅ Trademark landscape clear (nearest neighbour is SOTI, mobile device management — no collision). ✅ `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` all available.

### 12. Cinctis — platform

**The Metaphor:** The binding structure. Derived from the Latin *cincture* (to bind, encircle, or gird).
**The Pitch:** AI code generation is sprawling and chaotic. *Cinctis* binds it into a tight, secure, deliverable package.
**The Vibe:** Sharp and technical. It perfectly matches the existing Latin house style (*Omnia*, *Vectis*) while remaining highly ownable.

### 13. Telosiv (TEH-loss-iv) — org

**The Metaphor:** Greek *telos* — the end, the purpose, the completed state. In linguistics a *telic* verb has a built-in endpoint; generative AI is atelic — it can run forever without arriving anywhere.
**The Pitch:** Generation has no endpoint. *Telosiv* is the built-in arrival.
**The Vibe:** The purest *completion* story among the coined names — it names why the thread is cut rather than the cutting itself. Strong companion to Completiv.
**Clearance:** ❌ `.com` taken; ✅ `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` available (verified via `dig NS` + `whois`).

### 14. Claviv (KLAH-viv) — platform

**The Metaphor:** The key, twice over. Latin *clavis* — the key, the thing that grants governed access. And **Christopher Clavius**, the Jesuit mathematician behind the Gregorian calendar reform: the man who took a system that had drifted ten days out of true and brought it back into specification — with a correction mechanism so well-designed it has held for 450 years. (Clavius is also the moon base in *2001: A Space Odyssey*, where humanity quarantines and governs its first contact with a force it doesn't yet understand.)
**The Pitch:** Systems drift. *Claviv* is the correction that holds.
**The Vibe:** Precision, long-horizon maintenance, and quiet institutional authority — reform that outlasts its reformer. Sits naturally in the `-iv` family (Detentiv, Sottiv, Tersiv) and beside the Latin house style (Omnia, Vectis).
**Clearance:** ✅ `.com`, `.io`, and `.ai` all available (`.dev` needs manual confirmation) — one of the few candidates with a near-clean sweep. ⚠️ Flag for IP counsel: no direct collision found, but the `clav-` neighbourhood is busy with small software/security firms (Claviu, Clavi, Clavisi, Clavik, Clavium, Claviz, CLAIV) — the cluster needs a professional confusion-risk read.

### 15. Drystone (DRY-stone) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** The drystone wall. No mortar, no magic glue — well-chosen small stones placed deliberately by a craftsperson, and the structure holds a hillside for two hundred years. Structure from craft, not adhesive; small parts, carefully placed; outlasts its builder. Field walls also gate livestock — the boundary story comes free.
**The Pitch:** Everyone else pours glue. *Drystone* builds business software the way a waller builds: small pieces, deliberately placed, standing for decades.
**The Vibe:** Friendly, rural, durable — zero AI register; the only candidate in the Cursor/Linear register that survived the survey. Maps directly onto the small-model story (small stones, not monoliths) and the deterministic-governance story (gravity and fit, not adhesive hope). Engineers get a bonus wink: *Dhrystone* (the pun on Whetstone) is the classic 1984 CPU benchmark.
**Clearance:** ✅ **`.io` and `.dev` both verified available** (registry whois "0 objects" / Google-registry RDAP 404) — the only common word surveyed with two available exact TLDs. ❌ `.com` (UK holder) and `.ai` taken. ⚠️ Flag for IP counsel: no registered trademarks found anywhere; the name is used only by micro-scale UK companies (walling contractors, a community radio station, a Dorset IT consultancy, "Drystone IT Ltd" inc. Sept 2025, a Hull cyber outfit being struck off). The two small UK IT users are the confusion-risk read needed.

### 16. Excisiv (ek-SIZE-iv) — org

**The Metaphor:** Excision — the surgical cut that removes exactly what must go — fused with "decisive."
**The Pitch:** Generation is additive and messy. *Excisiv* is subtractive, deliberate, and final.
**The Vibe:** Sharp, active, highly technical. The one caution: it reads removal-first, where the mission is delivery-first.
**Clearance:** ✅ `.com`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev` all available (verified via `whois` "No match" + `dig NS`).

### 17. Lockgate (LOK-gate) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** The canal-lock gate. A lock doesn't dam the river or merely filter who passes — it lets a boat do something the raw river forbids: travel *uphill*, in bounded chambers, one gate sequence at a time, each transition deliberate and irreversible once the water levels. The lifecycle metaphor comes built in: chamber = slice, gate sequence = lifecycle transitions, lock-keeper = the operator at Gate 1.
**The Pitch:** The river only flows downhill. *Lockgate* is how your business climbs it.
**The Vibe:** Gating-as-advancement — the rare gate whose entire purpose is to take you higher. Friendly, spellable, and "lock" carries the security meaning for free.
**Clearance:** ✅ `.ai` and `.dev` verified available (registry whois "0 objects" / RDAP 404). ❌ `.com` and `.io` have active Cloudflare-hosted holders — flag for IP counsel to identify the users and read confusion risk.

### 18. Veritain (VEH-rih-tane) — org/platform

**The Metaphor:** *Veritas* (truth) + *-tain* (from *retinere*, to hold/maintain). "Holding the truth" — provenance, audit, and integrity maintained over time. Shares the exact cadence of *Oritain*, and `-tain` is the English face of *Tenere*, the top governance root in the `names-3.md` survey.
**The Pitch:** Generation forgets. *Veritain* holds the record true.
**The Vibe:** Institutional and steadfast, built on genuinely ancient stewardship verbs (maintain, sustain, retain). Hits the Fonterra/Oritain permanence target squarely while sitting naturally beside the Latin house style (Omnia, Vectis).
**Clearance:** ⚠️ `.com` registered since 2009 (Amazon Registrar, behind a whois proxy) — not immediately available. ⚠️ Flag for IP counsel: the `Veritai-` neighbourhood (one letter off) is busy, including **Veritai** (a Phoenix "Gen AI workspace and governance platform") in the exact category — a confusion-risk read is needed. No exact "Veritain" mark found.
